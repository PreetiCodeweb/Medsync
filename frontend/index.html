About MedSync

MedSync is a comprehensive hospital management platform designed to bridge the gap between patients and healthcare providers. Our AI-powered system ensures that patients receive timely and accurate medical guidance while hospitals can efficiently manage their resources.

👥 For Patients

Find nearby hospitals, check bed availability, consult with AI for symptom analysis, and get connected with the right medical specialists instantly.

🏥 For Hospitals

Manage hospital operations, track doctor availability, update bed status, and provide real-time information to patients seeking care.

🤖 AI Integration

Our RAG-powered system analyzes symptoms using a comprehensive medical knowledge base, providing intelligent recommendations and department matching.
